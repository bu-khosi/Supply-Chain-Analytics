Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing supply chain operations using Microsoft Power BI. It transforms supply chain data into interactive dashboards that help users understand sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, shipping efficiency and delivery performance.

The objective is to demonstrate practical Data Analytics skills through data preparation, data modelling, DAX calculations and interactive visualisation.

Tools and Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* Power Query
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* Data Modelling
* Data Visualisation

Project Objectives

* Analyse overall sales and profitability.
* Evaluate sales performance across product categories and regions.
* Understand customer purchasing patterns.
* Identify delivery delays and shipping risks.
* Compare shipping modes and delivery performance.
* Provide actionable business insights.

Dashboard Pages

1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of business performance, including total sales, orders, quantity, profit, regional performance and customer segments.

2. Shipping & Delivery Analysis

Examines delivery status, on-time delivery, late deliveries, shipping modes, average delivery times and late-delivery risk.

3. Product & Customer Insights

Analyses product sales, product profitability, top-performing products and customer distribution across customer segments.

Key Performance Indicators

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity
* Total Profit
* Average Shipping Days
* On-Time Delivery Rate
* Late Delivery Rate

Technical Skills Demonstrated

* Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query.
* Data modelling and table relationships.
* DAX measure creation.
* KPI development.
* Interactive dashboard development.
* Business performance analysis.
* Data storytelling and visualisation.

Business Value

The dashboards provide an interactive way to investigate supply chain performance, identify operational challenges, compare business segments and support data-driven decision-making.
